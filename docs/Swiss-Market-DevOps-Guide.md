# Swiss Market DevOps Guide

## Overview

This guide provides comprehensive insights into the Swiss DevOps job market, with **primary focus on consultancies** (PwC, Deloitte, Accenture) and secondary focus on tech companies, banking/fintech, and healthcare.

**Target Role**: Mid-Level Cloud DevOps Engineer
**Target Start**: June 1st, 2026
**Timeline**: Interview-ready in 2-3 weeks, offer by Week 8

---

## Industry Priority Rankings

### 1. Consultancies (PRIMARY TARGET) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why Consultancies?**:
- Multi-industry exposure (breadth over depth)
- Client-facing work (technical + communication skills valued)
- International work environment (English-primary)
- Higher salary potential (CHF 90k-120k for mid-level)
- Career growth (fast track to senior/lead roles)

| Company | Focus | Locations | Notes |

## Interview Process by Industry

### Consultancies (PwC, Deloitte, Accenture)

**Stages** (4-6 weeks total):
1. **HR Screening** (30 min)
   - Salary expectations
   - Availability
   - Basic qualifications check
2. **Technical Screen** (60 min)
   - Cloud platform knowledge (AWS/Azure)
   - IaC (Terraform scenarios)
   - Problem-solving (architecture questions)
3. **Case Study** (2-3 hours)
   - Client scenario: "Migrate legacy app to cloud"
   - Present architecture + cost estimate
   - Explain trade-offs to "client" (interviewer role-playing)
4. **Behavioral Interview** (60 min)
   - STAR format questions
   - Client communication scenarios
   - Multi-project juggling examples
5. **Partner Interview** (30-45 min)
   - Cultural fit
   - Long-term career goals
   - Salary negotiation

**What They Look For**:
- **Client Communication**: Can you explain Terraform to a CFO?
- **Structured Thinking**: Problem breakdown, systematic approach
- **Adaptability**: "Tell me about learning a new tech quickly"
- **Business Acumen**: Cost/benefit analysis, not just "it's technically cool"

**Preparation Tips**:
- Practice case studies (architecture design on whiteboard)
- Prepare consultancy-specific STAR stories (multi-client, tight deadlines)
- Research company's client portfolio (banking, healthcare, retail)

---

### Tech Companies (Google, Stripe, SIX)

**Stages** (3-5 weeks total):
1. **Recruiter Screen** (30 min)
   - Background check
   - Salary expectations
   - Motivation
2. **Technical Phone Screen** (45-60 min)
   - Kubernetes concepts (Pods, Deployments, Services)
   - Terraform code review (given sample, find issues)
   - CI/CD pipeline design
3. **On-Site/Virtual** (3-4 hours, multiple interviews):
   - **System Design** (60 min): "Design a scalable CI/CD system"
   - **Hands-On Coding** (60 min): Write Terraform module or Bash script
   - **Troubleshooting** (45 min): Debug failing K8s deployment (live)
   - **Behavioral** (45 min): STAR format, team collaboration
4. **Hiring Committee Review**
   - Feedback aggregated
   - Offer decision (1-2 weeks)

**What They Look For**:
- **Hands-On Skills**: Can you actually write Terraform, not just talk about it?
- **Problem-Solving**: Debugging approach (logs, metrics, systematic)
- **Scalability Mindset**: Design for 1M users, not 100
- **Cultural Fit**: "Move fast", ownership, blameless postmortems

**Preparation Tips**:
- Leetcode-style infrastructure problems (practice Terraform modules)
- Review Kubernetes troubleshooting commands (kubectl)
- Prepare KPZ architecture deep-dive (be ready to whiteboard)

---

### Banking/Fintech (UBS, Julius Baer, Swissquote)

**Stages** (4-8 weeks total, slower process):
1. **HR Screening** (30 min)
2. **Technical Interview 1** (60 min)
   - Security best practices (IAM, encryption, zero-trust)
   - Compliance awareness (FINMA, data residency)
   - Infrastructure design with regulatory constraints
3. **Technical Interview 2** (60 min)
   - Terraform/IaC deep-dive
   - Disaster recovery and backup strategies
   - Change management processes
4. **Behavioral Interview** (60 min)
   - Risk management examples
   - Handling production incidents under pressure
   - Attention to detail, thoroughness
5. **Team Interview** (45 min)
   - Cultural fit (conservative environment)
   - Long-term commitment

**What They Look For**:
- **Security Awareness**: Can you design zero-trust architecture?
- **Compliance Knowledge**: Understand FINMA, data residency, audit logs
- **Risk-Averse Mindset**: Thorough testing, rollback plans, DR procedures
- **Reliability**: "Tell me about a time you prevented an outage"

**Preparation Tips**:
- Research FINMA regulations (basics)
- Prepare security/compliance STAR stories
- Practice explaining complex security concepts simply

## Key Skills by Frequency

### Universally Required (95%+ of jobs)

1. **Cloud Platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP)**
   - AWS most common in tech/startups
   - Azure increasing (Microsoft partnerships)
   - GCP less common (except Google)

2. **Kubernetes**
   - 90% of jobs require K8s experience
   - EKS (AWS), AKS (Azure), GKE (GCP)
   - Helm, Kustomize (nice-to-have)

3. **Terraform / IaC**
   - 85% require Terraform specifically
   - Ansible declining
   - Pulumi emerging (code-first IaC)

4. **CI/CD**
   - GitHub Actions (tech companies)
   - GitLab CI (banking, healthcare)
   - Jenkins (legacy, but still common)

### Highly Valued (70%+ of jobs)

5. **Observability (DataDog/Prometheus/Grafana)**
   - DataDog (consultancies, tech companies)
   - Prometheus/Grafana (open-source preference)
   - CloudWatch (AWS-centric)

6. **Scripting (Python/Bash)**
   - Automation
   - Data processing
   - Glue code

7. **Containers (Docker)**
   - Prerequisite for Kubernetes
   - Local development

### Industry-Specific (50%+ in specific sectors)

8. **Security/Compliance**
   - Banking: FINMA, zero-trust, audit logs
   - Healthcare: GDPR, data anonymization
   - Consultancies: ISO 27001, multi-industry standards

9. **Networking**
   - VPC, subnets, routing
   - DNS, load balancers
   - (Less critical for consultancies, critical for banking)

## Relocation Considerations

### Berlin → Switzerland

**Visa (Non-EU Citizens)**:
- **B Permit**: Work permit (employer sponsors)
- **L Permit**: Short-term (<1 year)
- You likely have German residency → easier

**Cost of Living**:
- **Rent (Zürich)**: CHF 1,800 - 2,500/month (1-bedroom)
- **Health Insurance**: CHF 300 - 500/month (mandatory)
- **Food**: 50% more expensive than Berlin
- **Transport**: CHF 85/month (Zürich transit pass)
- **Taxes**: ~20-25% (lower than Germany)

**Net Salary Calculation** (CHF 100,000 gross):
- **Gross**: CHF 100,000
- **Taxes + Insurance**: ~CHF 25,000
- **Net**: ~CHF 75,000 (~EUR 82,000)
- **vs Berlin**: ~60% higher purchasing power

**Language**:
- **Work**: English (consultancies, tech companies)
- **Life**: Swiss German (Zürich dialect)
- You speak German (huge advantage for Swiss life, but work is English-primary in target companies)
